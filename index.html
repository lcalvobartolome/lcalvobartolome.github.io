Building language technologies that serve people

I design NLP-based tools that help us understand large document collections.

Hi, I'm Lorena :) So far my work has focused on topic models and scalable NLP systems that support real-world decisions, especially in the Science, Technology, and Innovation (STI) domain, where analysts need to understand large, messy document collections to guide decisions.

I'm now looking to extend this line of work into youth mental health, among other application areas. One direction I am aiming to explore is designing NLP-based tools that help practitioners identify which social and emotional factors drive risk in young people, and where timely interventions could prevent crises rather than just react to them, trying to answer questions such as "What factors, if changed, would have prevented a crisis?"

Research & selected publications

Methodologically, my research sits at the intersection of NLP and human-computer interaction. I've worked on making topic models and related tools more usable for end users: evaluation protocols that reflect real tasks, interactive systems for exploring large text collections, and interfaces that let experts guide models rather than just consume their output.
